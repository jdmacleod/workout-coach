## Sets / Reps
- Strength: 3-4x5-8
- Volume: 3x10-15

## Cues
Keep rings turned out at the bottom. Control the descent slowly. Lean forward slightly for more chest engagement.

## Progressions
- Beginner: Box-assisted ring dips or ring push-ups
- Advanced: Weighted ring dips, slow 3-sec eccentric

## Notes
More shoulder-friendly than bar dips due to the rotating grip. Core must stay tight throughout.
