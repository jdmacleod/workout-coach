name: Overhead Press
equipment: [barbell, bumper_plates]

difficulty: intermediate
---
## Sets / Reps
- Strength: 4x4-6 @ 75-85% 1RM
- Volume: 3x8-10 @ 65-70% 1RM

## Cues
Start from the floor (clean or deadlift to rack position). Brace core hard. Press bar straight up, head slightly back as bar passes face.

## Progressions
- Beginner: 3x8 @ empty bar, focus on bar path
- Advanced: Push-press for overload, then return to strict

## Notes
Seated on the floor (Z-press) for extra core demand and to remove leg drive.
