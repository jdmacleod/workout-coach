## Sets / Reps
- Strength: 4x6-10
- Hypertrophy: 3x12-15

## Cues
Lie on back, dumbbells at chest, elbows at ~45°. Press to lockout. Lower until elbows touch the floor — pause one second before pressing again.

## Progressions
- Beginner: 3x10 at comfortable weight
- Advanced: Slow 4-sec descent, pause at bottom

## Notes
No bench needed. The floor stop eliminates shoulder impingement risk. Great finishing accessory after barbell work.
