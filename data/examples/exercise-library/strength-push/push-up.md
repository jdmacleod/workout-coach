## Sets / Reps
- Volume: 3x15-20
- Strength: 4x8-12 (slow 3-sec descent)
- Density: 4 rounds max reps, 90-sec rest

## Cues
Hands shoulder-width, body in a straight plank line. Lower chest to floor. Full lockout at the top. No sagging hips.

## Progressions
- Easier: Incline push-up (hands on elevated surface)
- Harder: Deficit push-up (hands on books), archer push-up, one-arm assisted

## Notes
Foundational horizontal push. Infinite volume headroom — add reps, slow the tempo, or load via vest before progressing to harder variants.
