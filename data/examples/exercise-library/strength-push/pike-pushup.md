## Sets / Reps
- Volume: 3x10-15
- Strength: 4x6-8 (slow, 3-sec descent)

## Cues
Hips high, body inverted V-shape. Lower head toward floor between hands. Press back up, keeping elbows tracking slightly back.

## Progressions
- Easier: Regular push-up
- Harder: Feet elevated on chair or box, or deficit pike push-up

## Notes
Excellent shoulder press substitute needing zero equipment. Scales infinitely with foot elevation.
