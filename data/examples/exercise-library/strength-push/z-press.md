## Sets / Reps
- Strength: 4x4-6 @ 60-70% OHP 1RM
- Volume: 3x8-10

## Cues
Sit on the floor, legs straight out, barbell at shoulder height. Brace core hard — no back support. Press straight up. Keep torso perfectly vertical.

## Progressions
- Easier: Dumbbell Z-press (shorter ROM to establish)
- Advanced: Full barbell Z-press, timed holds at lockout

## Notes
Sitting removes leg drive and spinal support — weaknesses in core bracing or shoulder mobility show immediately. Lower loads than standing OHP but superior positional demand.
