## Sets / Reps
- Volume: 3x12-15
- Strength: 4x8-10 (heavy, slow descent)

## Cues
Hold dumbbell at chest. Feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out. Squat deep — elbows push knees out at bottom. Drive up through heels.

## Progressions
- Beginner: Box squat to ensure depth
- Advanced: Slow 4-sec descent, pause at bottom, single-leg variation

## Notes
Counterbalance makes an upright torso easy, teaching the squat pattern before loading heavier. Upper weight limit is your dumbbell — great for high rep accessory work when barbell is in use.
