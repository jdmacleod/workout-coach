## Sets / Reps
- Strength: 4x3-5 @ 80-90% 1RM
- Volume: 3x6-8 @ 70-75% 1RM

## Cues
Bar over mid-foot. Push the floor away, don't pull the bar up. Hips and shoulders rise together. Lock out with glutes, not hyperextending lower back.

## Progressions
- Beginner: Romanian deadlift to build hip hinge pattern first
- Advanced: Deficit deadlift, paused at knee, sumo stance

## Notes
Loaded from the floor every time (no rack). Use chalk or mixed grip at heavier loads. Treat every rep as a single — full reset of position before pulling.
