## Sets / Reps
- Volume: 3x10-12 each leg
- Strength: 4x6-8 each leg (heavier dumbbells)

## Cues
Rear foot elevated on a chair or surface. Front shin vertical at bottom. Lower until rear knee nearly touches floor. Drive through the front heel.

## Progressions
- Easier: Bodyweight to learn balance
- Advanced: Deficit (front foot elevated too), slow eccentric

## Notes
Most demanding single-leg movement available without a barbell rack. Balance is the limiter early — build that before adding load. Brutal on glutes and quads.
