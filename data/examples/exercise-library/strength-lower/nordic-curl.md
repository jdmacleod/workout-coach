## Sets / Reps
- Strength: 3x3-5 (brutal — progress slowly)
- Eccentric: 4x3 (slow 5-sec descent only, no concentric)

## Cues
Anchor feet under something heavy (sofa, bar under a door). Kneel tall. Lower your body forward with hamstrings fighting gravity. Catch yourself with hands if needed. Pull back up with hamstrings (or assist with arms).

## Progressions
- Beginner: Eccentric-only with hands catching fall
- Advanced: Full Nordic, weighted, deficit

## Notes
Highest hamstring injury prevention exercise in the evidence base. Brutally hard. Start with slow eccentrics and build over weeks. Any anchor that holds your ankles will work.
