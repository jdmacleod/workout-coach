## Sets / Reps
- Volume: 3x12-15
- Strength: 4x6-8 @ heavy

## Cues
Upper back against a stable surface (wall/box). Bar across hips with padding. Drive hips up until body forms a straight line. Squeeze glutes hard at the top. Control descent.

## Progressions
- Beginner: Bodyweight or dumbbell on hips
- Advanced: Pause at top, single-leg hip thrust

## Notes
Without a bench, use a wall or heavy box for back support. Best direct glute isolation exercise available. Load it — glutes respond to heavy weight.
