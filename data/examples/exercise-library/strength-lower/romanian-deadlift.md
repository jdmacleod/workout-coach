## Sets / Reps
- Volume: 3x10-12
- Strength: 4x6-8 @ 60-70% deadlift 1RM

## Cues
Start standing. Hinge at hips, push them back. Bar slides down legs. Feel hamstring stretch at bottom. Drive hips forward to return. Keep back flat throughout.

## Progressions
- Beginner: Bodyweight hip hinge to learn pattern
- Advanced: Single-leg RDL, pause at bottom

## Notes
The best hamstring builder accessible with just a barbell. Lower only until hamstrings are fully stretched — not to the floor unless you have exceptional flexibility.
