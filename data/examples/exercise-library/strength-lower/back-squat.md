## Sets / Reps
- Strength: 4x4-6
- Volume: 3x8-10

## Cues
Clean the bar and press it overhead, then lower to back-rack position. Brace hard. Descend below parallel. Drive through heels, chest up.

## Progressions
- Beginner: Goblet squat pattern first
- Easier main lift: Front squat (also cleanable without rack)

## Notes
No rack available — must clean the bar first. This caps max weight at clean ability. Front squat is often a better choice in this constraint. Save this for when the load fits within your clean capacity.
