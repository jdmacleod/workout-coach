## Sets / Reps
- Volume: 3x12-15 each leg
- Strength: 4x8-10 each leg (with dumbbells if available)

## Cues
Use a stable surface 40-60 cm high (box, chair, stairs). Step up fully, drive knee at the top. Lower slowly — don't crash down. Alternate legs or do all reps on one side.

## Progressions
- Easier: Lower box
- Advanced: Weighted step-ups, lateral step-ups, pause at top

## Notes
Underrated. Develops unilateral leg strength and balance without spinal loading. Pair a chair with dumbbells for challenging stimulus.
