## Duration
- Full: 8-10 min
- Pre-push session: 5 min

## Sequence
1. Arm circles — 15 forward, 15 backward
2. Cross-body stretch — 30 sec each side
3. Doorframe chest stretch — 30 sec each arm position (low, mid, high)
4. Sleeper stretch — 60 sec each side
5. Band pull-apart substitute: face-pull position with rings or doorframe

## Cues
Never force shoulder positions. Mild discomfort is ok; sharp pain is stop immediately. Sleeper stretch: lie on side, flex elbow 90°, gently push forearm toward floor.

## Notes
Do this before any pressing session. Shoulder impingement is largely preventable with consistent pre-work. The sleeper stretch dramatically improves internal rotation range in 2-4 weeks.
