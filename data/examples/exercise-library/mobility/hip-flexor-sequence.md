## Duration
- Full: 10-15 min (both sides)
- Quick: 5 min daily maintenance

## Sequence
1. Low lunge hold — 60 sec each side
2. 90/90 hip stretch — 60 sec each side
3. Pigeon pose — 90 sec each side
4. Hip circles — 10 each direction

## Cues
Breathe deeply throughout. Don't force range — relax into it. Posterior pelvic tilt in the lunge to get anterior hip stretch (don't just lean forward).

## Notes
Essential for desk workers and anyone doing heavy lower body training. Do this daily. Hip flexor tightness is the root cause of many lower back complaints.
