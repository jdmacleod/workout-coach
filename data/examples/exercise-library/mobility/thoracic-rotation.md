## Duration
- Full: 8-10 min
- Pre-workout: 3-5 min

## Sequence
1. Thread-the-needle — 10 reps each side
2. Book openings (lying) — 10 reps each side
3. Seated rotation over foam roller or rolled towel — 8 reps each side
4. Wall thoracic extension — 10 breaths

## Cues
Rotation should come from mid-back, not lower back. Relax the lower body completely. Exhale into rotation to get a little more range.

## Notes
Critical for overhead pressing, rowing, and avoiding shoulder impingement. Thoracic stiffness is rampant from desk posture. Even 5 minutes daily makes a measurable difference within 2-3 weeks.
