## Duration / Intensity
- Standard: 30-60 min @ Zone 2 heart rate (60-70% max HR, conversational pace)
- Build: Start 20 min, add 5 min per week

## Cues
Comfortable enough to hold a conversation. Nasal breathing preferred. Slow down if breathing through mouth exclusively. Flat terrain keeps intensity consistent.

## Progressions
- Easier: Zone 2 walk
- Advanced: Longer duration, mild hills, fasted state

## Notes
Foundation of aerobic fitness. Hard to do too much of this. 3+ hours per week has strong longevity data. Go slower than you think you need to.
