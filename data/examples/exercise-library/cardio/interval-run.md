## Duration / Intensity
- Classic: 8x400m @ 5k pace, 90-sec rest
- Tempo: 20 min continuous @ 80-85% max HR
- Fartlek: 30 min alternating easy/hard every 2-3 min by feel

## Cues
Hard intervals should feel genuinely hard — not sprint, but pushed. Rest intervals are active: walk or very easy jog. Don't start too fast; hold pace across all reps.

## Progressions
- Easier: Shorter intervals or longer rest
- Advanced: Shorter rest, more reps, longer distance per interval

## Notes
Builds VO2 max and running economy. Keep interval volume low (10-15% of weekly running) to avoid injury. Should leave you breathing hard but able to complete all reps.
