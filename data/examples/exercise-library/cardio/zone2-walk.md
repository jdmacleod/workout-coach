## Duration / Intensity
- Standard: 30-60 min at brisk pace
- Recovery: 20-30 min easy

## Cues
Brisk enough to raise heart rate but still fully conversational. Uphill walking raises intensity without running. Avoid phone-staring — this time is also for mental reset.

## Progressions
- Easier: Flat terrain, shorter duration
- Advanced: Hill walk, weighted vest, longer duration

## Notes
Underrated recovery and aerobic base tool. After intense training days, a 30-min brisk walk aids recovery without adding stress. Daily walks compound significantly over time.
