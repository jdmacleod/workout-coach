## Duration / Intensity
- Standard: 10 rounds: 10 burpees + 50-sec rest
- Endurance: 20 min AMRAP at sustainable pace
- Tabata: 8x (20 sec max burpees / 10 sec rest)

## Cues
Burpee = jump feet back to push-up, chest to floor optional, jump feet in, jump up with arms overhead. Pace yourself — early reps will feel easy; later ones won't.

## Progressions
- Easier: Step instead of jump (step-up burpee)
- Advanced: Add push-up, box jump instead of regular jump

## Notes
Equipment-free cardio that builds work capacity fast. Adjust rest ratios to control intensity. Excellent on travel days when nothing else is available.
