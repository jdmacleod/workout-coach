## Sets / Reps
- Volume: 3x12-15
- Strength: 4x6-8 (feet elevated, rings to chest)

## Cues
Hang under rings, body straight. Pull chest to rings, elbows back and tight. Pause at top. Lower under control.

## Progressions
- Easier: Higher body angle (more upright)
- Harder: Feet elevated, slow 4-sec descent, weighted vest

## Notes
Infinitely scalable — body angle controls intensity. Essential horizontal pulling movement when no barbell row is possible. Rings rotate to fit natural shoulder path.
