## Sets / Reps
- Strength: 3x6-8 each side (heavy, controlled)
- Conditioning: 3x10 each side (moderate)

## Cues
Start in push-up position with hands on dumbbells. Keep hips square to floor. Row one dumbbell to hip while stabilizing on the other. Lower and alternate.

## Progressions
- Easier: Wider stance (more stable base)
- Advanced: Add a push-up between each row

## Notes
Demands anti-rotation core strength. Hip rotation is cheating — keep pelvis still throughout. Heavy is relative here; a 10kg dumbbell becomes very challenging.
