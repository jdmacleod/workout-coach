## Sets / Reps
- Strength: 4x3-6 (weighted or slow)
- Volume: 4x6-10 bodyweight
- Grease-the-groove: 10 sets x 50% max throughout day

## Cues
Dead hang start. Pull elbows to ribs, chin over bar. Control the descent — 2-3 sec. No kipping unless programmed explicitly.

## Progressions
- Beginner: Ring rows, then jumping pull-ups with slow eccentric
- Advanced: Weighted pull-ups, L-sit pull-ups, archer pull-ups

## Notes
King of vertical pulling. Strict form builds connective tissue; kipping trades that for metabolic output. Both have their place — keep them distinct.
