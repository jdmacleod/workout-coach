## Sets / Reps
- Volume: 3x12-15 each side
- Strength: 4x8-10 each side (heavier, full range)

## Cues
Brace against a knee or surface. Pull elbow straight back — not flared out. Feel the lat initiate, not the bicep. Full stretch at bottom, squeeze at top.

## Progressions
- Beginner: Supported on a surface
- Advanced: Chest-supported on floor (prone), dead stop each rep

## Notes
Excellent unilateral work to correct left/right imbalances. Match volume and load between sides — never cheat the weaker side by using momentum.
