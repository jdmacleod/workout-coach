## Sets / Reps
- Accessory: 3x15-20
- Prehab: 2x20 (light, slow)

## Cues
Set rings at face height. Pull rings to temples with elbows high and wide — like a double bicep pose. External rotate at end range. Slow controlled return.

## Progressions
- Easier: Stand more upright (less bodyweight)
- Harder: More horizontal body angle

## Notes
Shoulder health essential. Program this with every push session to maintain rear delt and external rotator balance. Never skip it.
