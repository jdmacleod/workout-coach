## Sets / Reps
- Strength: 4x4-6 @ 70-80% 1RM
- Volume: 3x8-12

## Cues
Hinge to ~45° torso angle. Pull bar to lower chest/upper abdomen. Squeeze shoulder blades at the top. Keep lower back neutral throughout.

## Progressions
- Beginner: 3x8 with light weight, focus on scapular retraction
- Advanced: Pendlay rows (dead stop each rep), pause at top

## Notes
Loaded from the floor each set (no rack). Use a double-overhand grip for most sets; switch to mixed only at heavy weights for grip.
