## Sets / Reps
- Volume: 3x12-15
- Strength: 4x6-8 (feet elevated)

## Cues
Hang under bar, body straight. Pull chest to bar, keep elbows at ~45°. Squeeze shoulder blades at top. Lower fully before next rep.

## Progressions
- Easier: Higher bar angle
- Harder: Feet elevated on surface, weighted vest, slow eccentric

## Notes
Great stepping stone to pull-ups. Use a low bar or lower the pull-up bar to parallel. Same muscles as barbell row but horizontal and bodyweight-scalable.
