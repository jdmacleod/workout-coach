## Sets / Reps
- Conditioning: 4x5-8 @ moderate weight
- AMRAP: 10 min AMRAP at sustainable pace

## Cues
Start in push-up position with dumbbells. Push-up. Row right. Row left. Jump feet in. Clean to shoulders. Push-press overhead. Lower and repeat.

## Progressions
- Easier: Remove push-up or remove press
- Advanced: Heavier dumbbells, stricter press, reduce rest

## Notes
One of the most demanding combination movements. Combines push-up, bilateral row, clean, and press. With 10kg dumbbells this is still very challenging due to cardiovascular demand.
