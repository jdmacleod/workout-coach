## Sets / Reps
- Standard: 5 rounds: 5 pull-ups + 10 push-ups + 15 squats
- Classic: Cindy — 20 min AMRAP of that sequence
- Scaled: 3 rounds with rest between

## Cues
Move between exercises without rest within a round. Pull-ups strict. Push-ups full range. Squats below parallel. Rest 60-90 sec between rounds.

## Progressions
- Easier: Ring rows instead of pull-ups, reduce reps
- Advanced: Chest-to-bar pull-ups, diamond push-ups, jump squats

## Notes
Requires only a pull-up bar. The pull-up → push-up → squat combination hits entire body with minimal equipment. Scale reps to maintain quality throughout.
