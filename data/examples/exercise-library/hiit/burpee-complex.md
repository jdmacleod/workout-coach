## Sets / Reps
- Standard: 5 rounds: 10 burpees, 60-sec rest
- Escalating: 1-2-3-4-5-4-3-2-1 ladder
- Time: 10 min AMRAP

## Cues
Full range of motion: chest to deck, jump at the top with arm clap overhead. Keep hips from sagging on the way down. Breathe on the jump.

## Progressions
- Easier: No push-up, step instead of jump
- Advanced: Burpee pull-up (jump to bar), box jump, weighted vest

## Notes
Pure conditioning with zero equipment. Works anywhere. Humbling even for fit athletes. Great finisher or standalone session on minimal-equipment days.
