## Sets / Reps
- Conditioning: 4x10-15 @ moderate
- AMRAP: 21-15-9 with a couplet
- Tabata: 8x (20 sec max reps / 10 sec rest)

## Cues
Dumbbells at shoulders. Squat deep. Drive up and use the momentum to press overhead — it's one fluid movement. Punch through at the top.

## Progressions
- Easier: Squat and press separately
- Advanced: Heavier weight, faster cycling, barbell thrusters

## Notes
The gold standard conditioning movement. Heart rate spikes fast. Breathing is the limiter before legs or shoulders. Pair with pull-ups for a classic test workout.
