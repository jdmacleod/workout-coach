# Training Information

## About Me

Intermediate lifter, training 4 days per week for the past 2 years. Running base of
approximately 20–30 km per week. Currently healthy with no active injuries. Primary
goal is building upper body strength while maintaining aerobic fitness.

## Training Philosophy

I prefer balanced programming that mixes strength and conditioning. I respond well to
moderate volume (3–4 sets per main lift, 2–3 accessories). I need at least 2 rest days
per week to recover well. I enjoy variety but need enough repetition to track progress.

For strength work, I prefer a push/pull/legs split for upper body, with one full-body
or lower day mid-week. For cardio, I prefer zone 2 steady-state for aerobic base,
with one higher-intensity session per week maximum.

I train at home: barbell, rack, bench, pull-up bar, and kettlebells (16 kg, 24 kg).
Cardio options include outdoor running and rowing.

## Schedule Constraints

- Available days: Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Saturday, Sunday
- Max weekday session: 60 minutes
- Max weekend session: 90 minutes
- Must not train back-to-back more than 2 days in a row

## Example: Strength Upper Body (Push Focus)

Warm-up (5 min): band pull-aparts, arm circles, light overhead press

### Main Lifts
- Bench Press: 4×5 @ 80% 1RM
- Overhead Press: 3×8 @ 70% 1RM

### Accessories
- Pull-ups: 3×max (or 3×8 with band assist)
- Cable/band rows: 3×12
- Tricep dips: 3×10
- Face pulls: 3×15

### Notes
Target: 5–7 RPE on main lifts. Leave 2 reps in reserve. Focus on bar path and
controlled eccentric. Log all sets and weights.

## Example: Strength Lower Body

Warm-up (5 min): goblet squat, hip circles, glute bridges

### Main Lifts
- Back Squat: 4×5 @ 80% 1RM
- Romanian Deadlift: 3×8

### Accessories
- Bulgarian Split Squat: 3×8 per leg
- Nordic Curl or Leg Curl: 3×10
- Calf Raises: 3×15

### Notes
Rest 3 min between main lift sets. 90 sec between accessories.

## Example: Zone 2 Run

45 minutes at conversational pace. Heart rate target: 130–145 bpm.
Finish with 5 min walking cool-down. No pushing.

## Example: Mobility / Recovery

20 minutes of:
- Hip flexor stretch: 2 min per side
- Thoracic rotations: 10 reps per side
- Pigeon pose: 2 min per side
- Shoulder pass-throughs: 2×10
- Dead hangs: 3×20 sec
